# Grant writing resources

##### Training

UNM offers a number of research-related courses, many of them online. [Find a list of those here.](http://research.unm.edu/training-networking){target=_blank}

##### Find Funding

UNM's [Office of the Vice President for Research](http://research.unm.edu/){target=_blank} is a great starting point. UNM users can access [Pivot](http://frdo.unm.edu/funding_opportunities){target=_blank} to [search funding](http://research.unm.edu/find-funding){target=_blank}, which includes National Science Foundation and many other opportunities.

[Science Magazine](http://www.sciencemag.org/careers/where-search-funding){target=_blank} also has a list of funding resources.

##### Develop Proposal

Again, the OVPR offers [steps and tools](http://research.unm.edu/develop-proposal){target=_blank} to use in developing your proposal.

The tools available at the [Office for Sponsored Research](http://osp.unm.edu/index.html){target=_blank} website will help you develop budgets and contains lots of key information on rates and procedures.

[NSF Proposal and Award Policies and Procedures Guide](https://www.nsf.gov/pubs/policydocs/pappguide/nsf16001/gpg_print.pdf){target=_blank}

[NSF Guide for Proposal Writing](https://www.nsf.gov/pubs/2004/nsf04016/nsf04016.pdf){target=_blank}

##### Check Compliance

OVPR has a [list of items to check](http://research.unm.edu/ensure-compliance){target=_blank}, including Export Control.

##### Submit Proposal

OVPR gives you [the big picture view of the steps](http://research.unm.edu/submit-proposal){target=_blank} involved. Work with [OSP on specific questions about submitting your proposal](http://osp.unm.edu/index.html){target=_blank} through [Cayuse](http://osp.unm.edu/cayuse-research-suite.html){target=_blank}.

##### Additional Support

Faculty can seek additional support through the [Faculty Research Development Office.](http://frdo.unm.edu/){target=_blank}

[Advance](https://advance.unm.edu/){target=_blank} offers research development events and resources, with an emphasis on promoting and advancing women in STEM fields.

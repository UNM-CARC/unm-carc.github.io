# Grant writing resources

##### Describing CARC in your proposal

Proposals that will use CARC systems can cite the
[facilities description](/docs/about/facilities/) in the user documentation —
current cluster, storage, network, and data-center specifications written as
proposal boilerplate — and should [acknowledge CARC](publications.md) in
resulting publications. For help scoping the computing section of a proposal,
book a [consultation](../contact/office-hours.md).

##### Training

UNM's [Faculty Research Development Office](https://frdo.unm.edu/){target=_blank} (FRDO)
runs proposal-writing workshops and events, and
[Research Compliance](https://researchcompliance.unm.edu/training-and-resources/index.html){target=_blank}
provides the required conflict-of-interest, export-control, and research-integrity training.

##### Find Funding

FRDO's [funding opportunities](https://frdo.unm.edu/find-funding-opportunities.html){target=_blank}
page is the starting point; it covers UNM's access to the Pivot funding
database, which includes National Science Foundation and many other
opportunities. Departments can also draw on FRDO's
[research support directory](http://frdo.unm.edu/?q=dept_frdo_support){target=_blank}.

[Science Careers](https://www.science.org/careers/where-search-funding){target=_blank} also has a list of funding resources.

##### Develop Proposal

FRDO offers [proposal support](https://frdo.unm.edu/proposal-support.html){target=_blank} — steps, tools, and editing help for developing your proposal.

The [Office of Sponsored Projects](https://osp.unm.edu/){target=_blank} (OSP) website helps you develop budgets and holds key information on rates and procedures; see its [PI resources](https://osp.unm.edu/pi-resources/index.html){target=_blank}.

[NSF Proposal & Award Policies & Procedures Guide (PAPPG)](https://www.nsf.gov/policies/pappg){target=_blank}

[NSF Guide for Proposal Writing](https://www.nsf.gov/pubs/2004/nsf04016/nsf04016.pdf){target=_blank}

##### Check Compliance

UNM [Research Compliance](https://researchcompliance.unm.edu/){target=_blank} covers the items to check, including conflict of interest and research integrity. Export-controlled software or data on CARC systems needs prior written approval from UNM Export Control (<export@unm.edu>, 505-277-2968).

##### Submit Proposal

OSP's [procedures and guidelines](https://osp.unm.edu/pi-resources/procedures-and-guidelines.html){target=_blank} give the big-picture view of the steps involved; work with OSP on specific questions about submitting your proposal through Cayuse.

##### Additional Support

Faculty can seek additional support through the [Faculty Research Development Office](https://frdo.unm.edu/){target=_blank}.

[Advance](https://advance.unm.edu/){target=_blank} offers research development events and resources, with an emphasis on promoting and advancing women in STEM fields.

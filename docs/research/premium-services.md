# Premium services

To support users with needs consistently above the free baseline service, CARC has a UNM-approved cost model for charging for condo cluster nodes, VM hosting, dedicated storage, and custom system administration and application support. If you are not sure if you need to use the cost model, please **[contact us](mailto:unitadmin@carc.unm.edu)** for assistance.

Costs in this model are broken into three components:

1. **Capital hardware** is the cost of major equipment, and is excluded from F&A. These rates can be used to estimate expenses, but this will be billed based on actual cost from the vendor(s).
2. **Installation** is the cost of non-major equipment – things like racks, power supplies, and the like. Depending on the size of the purchase, some items in this category may be shared resources and are priced accordingly. As these items are tied to the capital equipment, this cost is also excluded from F&A.
3. **Service** is the cost of CARC staff required to administer systems or provide individualized support. Note service is subject to F&A if purchased via a grant; purchases made with unrestricted funds are not.

# Downtime notices

This page lists planned maintenance and service interruptions that may affect
CARC users. Details may change as work progresses; check back before a planned
maintenance window.

## Scheduled maintenance

### CARC system updates

**December 2-3, 2026**

CARC systems will be unavailable for two days while servers are updated.
Services are expected to be restored by the end of Thursday, December 3.

### Easley cooling-system repairs

**October 14-15, 2026**

Half of the Easley compute nodes will be unavailable while we perform repairs
on the cooling system.

### Firewall upgrade

**September 28th Starting at 3:00pm – September 30th, 2026**

Expect frequent and prolonged interruptions to network traffic to and from
CARC throughout the maintenance period. This will affect SSH logins, Open
OnDemand, the CARC Help Desk ticket system, ColdFront, and other
network-dependent services.

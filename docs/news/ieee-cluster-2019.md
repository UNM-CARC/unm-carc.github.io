# IEEE Cluster Conference comes to Albuquerque

<p class="carc-byline">2019</p>

![balloon fiesta albuquerque, NM Photo by Crawford Brian via photopin (CC license)](/assets/ieee-cluster-2019-cluster-comp.jpg)

*balloon fiesta albuquerque, NM Photo by Crawford Brian via photopin (CC license)*

The 2019 IEEE Cluster Conference will be held in Albuquerque, New Mexico from
September 23-27, 2019 at the Sheraton Uptown. This international conference,
sponsored by the Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers, serves as
a forum for sharing developments and accomplishments in the field of cluster
computing. Specific topics to be covered include applications, algorithms and
libraries; architecture, networks/communication, and management; programming
and systems software; and data, storage, and visualization.

The call for papers can be found on the [IEEE Cluster Conference
website](https://clustercomp.org/2019/technical/){target=_blank}, with
abstracts due May 10. A call for posters will be issued at a later date,
covering the same topics as noted in the call for papers. Registration
information will be available soon, [also on the
website](https://clustercomp.org/2019/registration/){target=_blank}. CARC
Director Prof. Patrick Bridges and Sandia Research and Development Manager Ron
Brightwell are the general co-chairs of the 2019 edition of the conference.

IEEE is “the world’s largest technical professional organization dedicated to
advancing technology for the benefit of humanity,” according to [their
website](https://www.ieee.org/about/vision-mission.html){target=_blank}. IEEE
has over 423,000 members in more than 160 countries globally.

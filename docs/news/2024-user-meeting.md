# Registration is open for the 2024 CARC user meeting

<p class="carc-byline">By Mariah Rosales · March 2024</p>

![](/assets/2024-user-meeting.jpg)

The UNM Center for Advanced Research Computing (CARC) will hold its [third
annual User Meeting](/education/workshops/) on April 16, 2024 at Zimmerman
Library in the technology enabled B30, located in the basement. [Registration
is open
now!](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSfqoReC0h_Nc366kom2MDQAR7Rbrenddm0CHEBMO1W9oHMLmw/viewform?usp=sf_link){target=_blank}

The annual User Meeting is held in hopes to establish a platform for
meaningful exchanges among existing users and center staff. Additionally, the
event seeks to foster interactions and the exchange of ideas among users
across various disciplines, with valuable feedback gathered from the user
community to continue improving CARC.

Last year’s implementation of a student poster session was a success, and we
will be continuing the idea into this year's meeting. We are accepting poster
submissions until our deadline for registration on April 5th. As always, we
encourage researchers, particularly students, to [submit their abstracts when
registering](/education/workshops/) so they can present their posters in the
afternoon poster reception at the User Meeting. *Posters will need to be 36” x
48”.*

This year’s event will feature CARC updates discussed over a luncheon, keynote
and researcher discussions, and an open discussion about the current research
computing needs of users followed by the poster session which will include
coffee and snacks. Similar to last year, the poster session will showcase
student and postdoctoral work utilizing computing resources in various ways.

**Registration will close Friday, April 5. Lunch will be provided!** We extend
an invitation to all those with an interest in research computing to join us
and discover more about the Center, along with the resources it offers to the
UNM community.

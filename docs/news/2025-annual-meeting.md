# Registration is open for the 2025 CARC Annual Meeting (CAM)

<p class="carc-byline">By Mariah Rosales</p>

The UNM Center for Advanced Research Computing (CARC) is excited to announce
its fourth annual user meeting, now called the CARC Annual Meeting (CAM),
scheduled for April 14, 2025.

CAM serves as a platform for meaningful exchanges between users and center
staff, while also fostering interdisciplinary collaboration and innovation.
This event provides an opportunity for researchers across various fields to
share insights, provide feedback, and contribute to the ongoing development
of CARC's computing resources.

![Attendees at the 2024 CARC user meeting](https://carc.unm.edu/images/2024-carc-user-meeting.png)

Building on the success of last year's student poster session, we are
thrilled to continue this tradition. We encourage students and researchers
to submit abstracts when registering to showcase their work at the afternoon
poster reception. Posters should be 36" × 48".

This year's event featured updates on CARC initiatives over a luncheon, a
keynote presentation, researcher discussions, and an open forum to discuss
current research computing needs. The day concluded with the poster session,
accompanied by coffee and snacks, where students and postdoctoral
researchers presented their computational research projects.

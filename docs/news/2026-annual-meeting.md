# Registration is open for the 2026 CARC Annual Meeting

<p class="carc-byline">By Mariah Rosales</p>

The UNM Center for Advanced Research Computing (CARC) will host its annual
**CARC Annual Meeting (CAM)** on Tuesday, April 21, 2026 at Zimmerman Library
in the technology-enabled B30, located in the basement.
[Registration is now open!](https://forms.office.com/Pages/ResponsePage.aspx?id=MJiqJfngK0iJfho7PIVeXIwY0OuI-zpOi4KHv9sI6KdUQjdMQVUwS1c2VUZKREoyTk9XT1NXRkFZTC4u){target=_blank}

CAM brings together members of the CARC community for a day of updates,
discussion, and research highlights from across the university. The meeting
provides an opportunity for researchers, students, and staff to connect with
CARC personnel, learn about the latest developments in the center, and share
how high-performance computing is supporting research across a wide range of
disciplines.

Building on the success of previous meetings, this year's event will once
again include a **student poster session**. Students and researchers are
encouraged to submit posters when registering to share how they are using
CARC resources in their work. The poster session offers a chance to showcase
ongoing projects, spark conversations, and connect with other members of the
research computing community at UNM.

The meeting will also feature updates from CARC staff and discussions about
the current and future role of research computing at the university. The
event is designed to foster collaboration between users and the center while
providing a space for the community to exchange ideas and feedback.

The 2026 CARC Annual Meeting will take place from **11am–3pm on Tuesday,
April 21, in Zimmerman Library B30**, with lunch provided for attendees.
Additional details, including the meeting agenda, will be shared soon.

Those interested in attending or presenting a poster can
[register here](https://forms.office.com/Pages/ResponsePage.aspx?id=MJiqJfngK0iJfho7PIVeXIwY0OuI-zpOi4KHv9sI6KdUQjdMQVUwS1c2VUZKREoyTk9XT1NXRkFZTC4u){target=_blank}!

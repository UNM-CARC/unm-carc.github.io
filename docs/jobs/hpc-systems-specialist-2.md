## The role

Support CARC's production HPC environment, focusing on storage and data
infrastructure alongside Ethernet and InfiniBand networks. Work spans parallel
filesystems, Linux systems, hardware, firmware, and troubleshooting across
these components.

## What you bring

* A bachelor's degree and at least three years of directly related experience.
  Additional qualifying degrees may substitute for experience year for year.
* Preferred experience includes parallel filesystems such as GPFS, BeeGFS, or
  Lustre; data platforms such as iRODS, CyVerse, or Kubernetes; high-speed
  networking; Linux administration; Slurm; and scripting or automation.

Candidates need not know every listed technology. See the official posting
for the complete qualifications and responsibilities.

## Research Assistants

The Center for Advanced Research Computing has many exciting opportunities for
bright, highly motivated graduate and undergraduate students to participate in
research projects in high performance computing, cloud computing, networking,
artificial intelligence, and applied science. Research Assistants at CARC work
closely with UNM faculty and CARC research staff to address the most
challenging problems, at the forefront of science and engineering, using
state-of-the-art computing and data science techniques. Specialized training in
high performance computing, artificial intelligence, and a generous stipend,
including tuition remission and fee waivers, are provided. Students with
expertise in C, C++, MPI, Java, Linux, Python, Rust, and web are especially
encouraged to apply. Positions are highly competitive and early application is
recommended.

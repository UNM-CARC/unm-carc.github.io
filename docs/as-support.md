# A&S Support

## Mission

A&S Support advances research computing for the UNM School of Arts & Sciences.
Its work includes Linux system support and administration, along with
scientific and research computing involving AI, cloud services, and
departmental computational resources.

## Current departmental support

We currently support research-computing needs in:

* Chemistry
* Mathematics and Statistics
* Earth and Planetary Sciences
* Physics and Astronomy
* Biology

## Get support

We hold regular office hours in the departments we support. To submit a support
ticket, email [researchtech@unm.edu](mailto:researchtech@unm.edu).

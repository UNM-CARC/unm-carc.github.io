# Program specializations

The CSE certificate can be completed in a number of different fields and
areas of interest. Below are our most commonly requested specializations.

!!! note

    Specializations are not transcripted — your transcript reflects the
    certificate itself.

Both specializations are ways of satisfying the certificate's
[requirements](requirements.md): M.S. and post-degree students complete
3 credit hours of core courses plus 9 credit hours of electives (four
courses); Ph.D. students complete 6 core plus 9 elective hours (five courses);
and every student completes the research project requirement — a thesis or
dissertation with a significant computational component, or an advanced
computational project.

!!! warning "Course numbers carried over from the previous website"

    The [core courses](requirements.md#core-courses) and
    [approved electives](electives.md) were checked against the UNM Schedule
    of Classes for Fall 2026, but the specialization-specific courses below
    (ME 530, ME 535, ME 561, MATH 311/312/314/316/321, CS 521, CS 529,
    STAT 527/528, and the 500-level statistics options) were not, and some
    have been renumbered since these plans were written. Confirm availability
    and current numbering with the Program Advisor (<csecert@carc.unm.edu>)
    before planning a sequence. Courses that are not on the approved-electives
    list count toward the certificate through the substitution petition
    described under [Electives](requirements.md#electives).

## Computational Fluid Dynamics

**Prerequisites**: MATH 311; MATH 314 or MATH 321; and MATH 312 or MATH 316.
The half-day *Introduction to Computing at CARC* workshop, offered every
semester, is recommended — see
[workshops](/docs/training/workshops/) in the user documentation.

### M.S. students and the post-degree certificate

* CS 471 / MATH 471, *Introduction to Scientific Computing* (core)
* ME 535 (engineering majors; offered in fall semesters of odd-numbered years)
  or ME 530 (Math/CS majors)
* Two approved electives, for example CS 542, MATH 504, MATH 505, MATH 576,
  MATH 578, or ME 561
* Thesis or approved independent study

Suggested sequence:

| | Fall | Spring |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Year 1 | CS/MATH 471; ME 535 (engineering majors) or ME 530 (Math/CS majors) | Elective 1 |
| Year 2 | Elective 2 | Thesis or project |

### Ph.D. students

* CS 471 / MATH 471 and CS 542, *Introduction to Parallel Processing* (core)
* ME 535 (engineering majors; fall semesters of odd-numbered years) or ME 530
  (Math/CS majors)
* Two approved electives, for example MATH 504, MATH 505, MATH 576, MATH 578,
  or ME 561
* Dissertation

Suggested sequence:

| | Fall | Spring |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Year 1 | CS/MATH 471; ME 535 (engineering majors) or ME 530 (Math/CS majors) | Elective 1 |
| Year 2 | CS 542; Elective 2 | Elective 2 (may be taken in either the fall or the spring of year 2) |

## Data Science

### M.S. students and the post-degree certificate

* CS 567, *Principles and Applications of Big Data* (core)
* STAT 527 (non-Math/Stat majors) or STAT 528 (Math/Stat majors)
* Two pre-approved electives: one of CS 542, CS 521, or CS 529, and one
  500-level statistics course such as STAT 540, STAT 545, STAT 547, or
  STAT 577 (see the [UNM Catalog](https://catalog.unm.edu/){target=_blank})

Suggested sequences:

| Non-Math/Stat majors | Fall | Spring |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Year 1 | CS 567; STAT 527 | Elective 1 |
| Year 2 | Elective 2 | — |

| Math/Stat majors | Fall | Spring |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Year 1 | CS 567 | STAT 528 |
| Year 2 | Elective 1 | Elective 2 |

### Ph.D. students

* CS 567 and CS 542 (core)
* STAT 527 (non-Math/Stat majors) or STAT 528 (Math/Stat majors)
* Two pre-approved electives: one of CS 521 or CS 529, and one 500-level
  statistics course such as STAT 540, STAT 545, STAT 547, or STAT 577

Suggested sequences:

| Non-Math/Stat majors | Fall | Spring |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Year 1 | CS 567; STAT 527 | Elective 1 |
| Year 2 | CS 542; Elective 2 | — |

| Math/Stat majors | Fall | Spring |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Year 1 | CS 567 | STAT 528 |
| Year 2 | CS 542; Elective 1 | Elective 2 |

CS 567 and CS 542 typically alternate semesters (neither is offered in Fall
2026), so check the [core course schedule](requirements.md#core-courses) when
choosing which year to start.

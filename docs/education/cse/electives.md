# Electives and participating departments

The following courses are officially approved electives for the CSE Program.
Each semester, certain special topics courses may also be approved for
credit, following evaluation by the CSE Program Director. See the UNM
[Course Catalog](https://catalog.unm.edu/){target=_blank} for course
descriptions and the
[Schedule of Classes](https://schedule.unm.edu/){target=_blank} for what is
offered in a given semester.

!!! note "Offering status, checked August 30, 2026"

    Every course below was checked against the UNM Schedule of Classes for
    **Fall 2026** (✅ = sections scheduled on main campus; — = no Fall 2026
    sections). Click a course number to open its live schedule entry. Many
    electives run in alternating semesters, so — does not mean a course is
    discontinued; the **Spring 2027 schedule publishes in mid-October 2026**.
    Questions about a specific course or a substitution: <csecert@carc.unm.edu>.

!!! warning "Some course numbers are changing"

    The Fall 2026 schedule lists several graduate courses under new numbers
    and titles (for example, graduate Computer Networks now appears as CS
    585, Machine Learning as ECE 517, and numerical methods offerings under
    revised MATH numbers). The CSE Program Committee is reviewing this list
    against the 2026–2027 catalog — confirm your program of study with a
    Program Advisor before enrolling.

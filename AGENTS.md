# unm-carc.github.io

Follow the editing conventions of the UNM-CARC docs repository: https://github.com/UNM-CARC/docs/blob/main/AGENTS.md

This repo is the source of https://carc.unm.edu/ and, as the org ROOT Pages site (it must stay named unm-carc.github.io), its staging mirror at https://unm-carc.github.io/. Every content page needs OKF v0.2 frontmatter with a non-empty type; section index.md files carry no frontmatter; log.md is the dated change log. News pages also carry `date:` (YYYY, YYYY-MM or YYYY-MM-DD); the Archive in docs/news/index.md between the `archive:start`/`archive:end` markers is generated from it by scripts/port_legacy_pages.py (`--offline --no-rewrite`), never edited by hand. Stories ported from the old site are described in migration/pages.yml.

Build order: scripts/okf_validate.py docs, scripts/check_legacy_links.py docs, scripts/gen_llms_txt.py, scripts/build_cascade_site.py, scripts/postbuild_agent_surface.py site, scripts/gen_htaccess.py site. Zensical does not render this site; zensical.toml only supplies site_url (CARC_SITE_URL overrides it).

Publishing: CI pushes the built site into Cascade CMS through scripts/cascade_sync.py and Cascade publishes it by SFTP; never edit in Cascade. Images go in docs/assets/ and are referenced as /assets/<name> — never hotlink carc.unm.edu. A page's sources[].resource carc.unm.edu URL becomes a 301 to that page automatically. `layout: cards` in frontmatter renders a #### [Title](url) + image + summary list as a card grid.

## Homepage news ticker and downtime notices

The homepage ticker is generated by `scripts/news_ticker.py` during the Cascade
build. Its news headlines come from the current-story links **above**
`archive:start` in `docs/news/index.md` (up to six, newest `date` first). Add new
stories there; do not hardcode ticker headlines into `web/index.html`.

Maintain downtime notices in `docs/downtime-notices.md` in **both** places:
the readable body and the frontmatter `notices` list. Each entry needs:

```yaml
notices:
  - anchor: carc-system-updates
    title: "CARC system updates: December 2–3"
    status: scheduled
    start: "2026-12-02T00:00:00-07:00"
    end: "2026-12-04T00:00:00-07:00"
```

* `anchor`: unique, stable lowercase ID; put a matching
  `<span id="carc-system-updates"></span>` immediately before its body heading.
  Keep the ID when editing a notice so existing ticker links still work.
* `title`: short ticker headline including the affected systems and dates.
* `status`: `scheduled`, `active`, or `resolved`. Scheduled entries require
  `start` and `end`; use ISO timestamps with Albuquerque's correct UTC offset
  (`-07:00` MST or `-06:00` MDT). `end` is **exclusive**: maintenance through
  December 3 ends at midnight starting December 4.
* Use `active` for an ongoing or unexpected interruption; its ticker entry stays
  until staff mark it `resolved`, even if its expected end passes. Update the
  body as well. Do not mark an outage resolved merely because its window ended.
* The ticker displays active/current notices first, then the nearest future
  scheduled window, then current news. Past scheduled and resolved notices are
  omitted. Browser filtering refreshes each minute, so expired scheduled entries
  disappear without a rebuild; changing notice text/status still requires publish.
* Keep historical notice text on the page. Do not parse human-readable dates or
  add a separate ticker flag; the structured entries are the ticker's source.

After changing ticker code, run `python -m unittest discover -s tests` and
`node --test tests/news-ticker.test.cjs`, in addition to the normal build checks.
